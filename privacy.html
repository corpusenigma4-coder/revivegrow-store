Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ReviveGrow ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit our website or place an order. We are committed to handling your data responsibly and in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Information we collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect: your name, email address, delivery address, phone number (optional) and order details. Payments are processed securely by PayPal — we do not store your full card details on our servers.

2. How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To send order confirmations, tracking and service updates
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide customer support
  • To handle returns, refunds and our 90-day guarantee
  • To improve our website and product offering

3. Legal basis for processing

We process your data to perform our contract with you (fulfilling orders), to meet our legal obligations, and where you have given consent (for example, marketing emails, which you can opt out of at any time).

4. Sharing your information

We only share your data with trusted third parties needed to run our store — such as payment providers (PayPal) and delivery carriers. We never sell your personal data to anyone.

5. Data retention

We keep your order information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described above and to comply with legal, accounting and reporting requirements.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to processing or request data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies to make the site work and basic analytics to understand how visitors use it. You can control cookies through your browser settings.
